I'm **Shukai Liu (刘书恺)**, also known as **Shark Liu**, a Ph.D. student at the [School of Computer Science and Engineering](https://scse.buaa.edu.cn/), [Beihang University](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/), advised by Prof. **Bo Jiang** and Prof. **Jian Yang**. I joined Beihang in 2024 as a master's student under Prof. **Zhoujun Li** and transferred to the Ph.D. program in 2025. Before that, I received my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology from [Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications](https://www.bupt.edu.cn/) in 2024 (GPA 3.83/4.00, ranked 14/442).

My research focuses on **code large language models** and **software engineering (SWE) agents**, including:
- **Scalable SWE data**: executable multilingual SWE environments, trajectory distillation and trajectory selection (SWE-Crafter, SWE-Slim);
- **Agent training**: context management for long-horizon agents and harness-invariant agent training (CaT, HINT);
- **Code evaluation**: multilingual benchmarks for code generation and debugging (McEval, MdEval).

I am also a core contributor to [IQuest-Coder-V1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16733) and [IQuest-Q1](https://huggingface.co/IQuestLab/IQuest-Q1) at IQuest Research, where I work on SWE-oriented evaluation, data and post-training. Feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:Liusk@buaa.edu.cn) for collaboration or discussion.
